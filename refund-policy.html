REFUND
POLICY.

The cancellation and refund framework for Orvix Media's digital and electronic services, including websites, SEO, media, editorial, design and publishing work.

Last updated: 5 October 2026. This page is written for Orvix Media's digital services and should be read together with any project-specific quotation, invoice or written agreement.

1. Digital Services

All services provided by Orvix Media are digital/electronic services, and no physical goods are supplied. Because services can involve time, labour, research, writing, design, development, editing, scheduling and platform preparation, refund eligibility depends on the stage and nature of the service.

2. Cancellation Before Work Starts

A client may request cancellation within seven (7) days of making the payment. If the cancellation request is received within this seven-day period, the maximum refund available is 50% of the amount paid. The remaining 50% may be retained to cover administrative, scheduling, payment-processing and service-allocation costs. Eligibility remains subject to verification of the payment and cancellation request.

3. Cancellation After Work Starts

After seven (7) days from the date of payment, the payment is non-refundable, regardless of whether the service is still in progress or has not yet been delivered. Once the service has been completed, delivered, published, substantially performed or otherwise processed, it is also non-refundable.

4. Completed or Delivered Services

Payments for services that have already been completed, published, delivered, approved or substantially performed are generally non-refundable. This includes digital files, completed website work, published editorial features, magazine layouts, creative assets and other completed deliverables.

5. Media & Editorial Publishing

For media coverage, recognition features, editorial publishing and magazine services, refund requests after drafting, design, production, approval, scheduling or publication may be declined because the service involves work and digital publication activity that cannot be returned like a physical product.

6. Website & Development Services

For website development and technical projects, refund eligibility may depend on the amount of development already completed, project setup, purchased third-party resources, design work, development work, integrations and other committed costs. Completed work may be billed or retained against the amount already paid.

7. SEO & Ongoing Services

SEO and ongoing optimisation involve continuing professional work rather than a physical deliverable. Fees relating to work already performed or a service period already commenced are generally non-refundable. Future work may be cancelled subject to the agreed engagement terms.

8. Revision & Change of Mind

A client changing its preferred design, content, headline, branding direction or business decision after approving work does not automatically create a right to a refund. Reasonable revisions are handled according to the agreed scope; substantial changes may require additional charges.

9. Client-Side Delays

A refund is not automatically due where a project is delayed because the client has not supplied required information, approvals, assets, credentials or feedback. Orvix Media may pause the project until the required inputs are received.

10. Duplicate or Incorrect Payments

If a client accidentally makes a duplicate payment or an incorrect payment is clearly identifiable, the client should contact Orvix Media promptly. After verification, an eligible excess amount may be refunded using an appropriate payment method, subject to applicable payment-processing charges and provider rules.

11. Approved Exceptions

In exceptional circumstances, Orvix Media may voluntarily approve a partial or full refund where it considers that appropriate. Any such approval is case-specific and does not create an automatic entitlement for future projects.

12. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, processing time may depend on the payment method, bank, card issuer, payment gateway or other financial institution involved. Orvix Media will initiate the approved refund through the appropriate available method and cannot control the processing time imposed by third parties.

13. How to Request a Refund

Refund or cancellation requests should be sent to Info@theorvixmedia.com with the client's name, service/project details, payment date, transaction reference where available, and the reason for the request. Requests should be made as soon as the issue becomes known.

14. Disputes

Before initiating a payment dispute or chargeback, clients are requested to contact Orvix Media so the matter can be reviewed and resolved where possible. Nothing in this policy limits any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

15. Policy Changes

Orvix Media may update this Refund Policy when its service model or payment processes change. The version applicable to an engagement may also be determined by the terms accepted at the time the service was purchased.

15A. Service Timeline & Delivery Date

Services are provided on an advance-payment basis unless otherwise agreed in writing. The normal service timeline is approximately two weeks; however, some cases may take two weeks or longer. Completion depends on the service scope, production requirements, client responses, approvals, corrections, required information and other project-specific circumstances.

Orvix Media does not guarantee or promise a fixed delivery date unless a specific date has been expressly confirmed in writing. A stated or estimated timeframe is not a guaranteed delivery date.

16. Contact

For cancellation, refund or privacy-related queries, contact Info@theorvixmedia.com or +91-9992363027. Orvix Media is based in Rohtak, Haryana, India.
