Frequently AskedQuestions

Media coverage begins with your profile, company information, achievements, services, expertise, or the story you want to communicate. We organise the information into an editorial structure, prepare the feature draft, refine the presentation, and format it for the relevant platform. Features are published on our own platforms, TheCEOVerse, TheExecutiveTimes and InsightForgeMedia, which are owned and operated by Orvix Media. The process can include editorial writing, profile development, headline and section structuring, image selection, and final formatting, with the required details reviewed before the feature is finalised or published.

Yes. TheCEOVerse, TheExecutiveTimes and InsightForgeMedia are our own platforms. We run them end to end, from editorial and design to development and publishing. Any service offered on these platforms is handled by Orvix Media, and payments for those services are collected directly by Orvix Media.

The editorial draft is built from the information you provide, such as your professional background, company story, achievements, areas of expertise, services, milestones, website, social links, photographs, and any specific points you want highlighted. We turn that material into a clear, structured feature rather than simply placing the supplied information on a page. Once the draft is prepared, the relevant details can be reviewed so names, designations, company information, achievements, and other important facts are presented correctly before finalisation.

Magazine designing covers the complete visual presentation of a publication, including the cover, typography, page layouts, image placement, section hierarchy, editorial formatting, and overall visual consistency. We organise the approved content into a readable magazine structure and create layouts that work across the required pages and formats. The process can also include cover concepts, feature-page layouts, contents pages, profile sections, advertisement or promotional pages, and final export preparation, with revisions handled according to the agreed scope.

Yes. Before final delivery or publishing, the relevant draft or design can be reviewed for factual details, names, titles, company information, images, page layouts, and other required corrections. This review stage helps ensure that the approved information is represented correctly and that the final editorial or magazine design matches the agreed direction. The number and scope of revisions can depend on the project or service agreed at the beginning.

Website development starts with understanding your business, audience, content, required pages, functionality, and visual direction. We can work on business websites, portfolio websites, media and magazine websites, landing pages, WordPress websites, and custom web experiences, with responsive layouts designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens. The process can include page structure, UI design, front-end development, CMS or WordPress implementation, content sections, forms, performance considerations, testing, and launch support depending on the project scope.

SEO can cover technical improvements, on-page optimisation, keyword research, content structure, metadata, internal linking, image optimisation, website performance, and other search-related improvements based on the website and its goals. We first look at the existing site structure and content to identify areas that can be improved, then work on relevant optimisation and content recommendations. SEO is an ongoing process, so results can depend on the website, competition, content quality, search demand, and the consistency of optimisation over time.

Brand and creative design can cover logo design, brand identity, visual direction, social media creatives, digital graphics, magazine designing, editorial layouts, and other branded communication materials. We develop a visual system around the brand so that typography, colours, layouts, imagery, and graphic elements work consistently across different platforms. For magazine and editorial projects, the design can extend from the cover and feature pages to contents, profiles, promotional sections, and final publication-ready layouts.

Yes. These services can be planned as separate projects or combined when a brand needs a broader digital presence. A single project can bring together website development, brand and creative design, magazine designing, SEO, content, media coverage, editorial publishing, and digital marketing so that the website and communication remain consistent. The exact combination, timeline, deliverables, and pricing depend on the scope and requirements discussed before the project begins.
